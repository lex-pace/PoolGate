# Code of Conduct

PoolGate is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

Expected behavior:

- communicate respectfully and assume good faith;
- focus criticism on ideas, code, and behavior rather than people;
- accept constructive feedback;
- protect private information and never publish credentials or personal data;
- help keep discussions technically useful and inclusive.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, and publishing someone else's private information without permission.

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, issues, commits, and contributions that violate this policy. Report serious or private incidents through the security contact in `SECURITY.md` rather than posting sensitive details publicly.
